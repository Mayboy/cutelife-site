CUTE Life 是給中國科技大學（China University of Technology, Taiwan）學生使用的非官方校園 App：課表、成績、缺曠、請假、Moodle 作業與校園公告，一次看清楚。

CUTE Life is an unofficial companion app for students of China University of Technology (Taiwan): timetable, grades, attendance, leave requests, Moodle homework and campus announcements in one place.

本 App 並非中國科技大學官方出品。 This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by China University of Technology.
